The 7 Best THC Drinks for Sipping, Mixing, & Sharing in 2026

Good drinks. Good vibes. No booze required.

The THC drink aisle has gotten crowded… and that’s a good thing!

Today, you can find everything from THC-infused iced teas, cocktail-inspired drinks, spirit alternatives, pocket-sized mixers, and enough flavors and formats to stock a bar cart—without any alcohol.

So, naturally, we did some drinking!

We sipped our way through some of this year’s most interesting THC beverages, looking for drinks that delivered on flavor, experience, and the all-important question: Would we actually reach for another one?

From a Southern-inspired iced tea to an on-the-go mixer, here are seven standout THC drinks for every kind of sipper.

Best Crowd-Pleaser

Cornbread Hemp Blueberry Breeze THC Seltzer

Photography courtesy of Cornbread Hemp

If you’re filling the cooler for a group, Blueberry Breeze is the can we’d throw in first!

Cornbread Hemp keeps this one simple: bright blueberry flavor, a crisp sparkling finish, and up to 10mg of hemp-derived THC per can. It’s fruity and light, not syrupy, and familiar enough to appeal to the friend who hasn’t quite figured out the whole THC beverage thing yet.

And the judges agree! Blueberry Breeze picked up Gold at the High Spirits Awards. Easy-drinking, approachable, and only 25 calories per can—this is the one we’d expect to disappear from the cooler first.

Best Variety Pack

Cornbread Hemp 5mg THC Seltzer Variety Pack

Photography courtesy of Cornbread Hemp

Commitment issues? Yeah, us too.

Cornbread Hemp’s Variety Pack puts four delicious, award-winning flavors in one box: Blueberry Breeze, Raspberry Limeade, Peach Iced Tea, and Salted Watermelon. Better yet, all four actually taste different, rather than feeling like variations on the same generic fruit seltzer.

Each can contains 5mg of hemp-derived THC and 30 calories or less, putting them right in the sweet spot for casual hangs, cookouts, or stocking the cooler for friends with wildly different flavor preferences.

Our advice: try all four before picking a favorite. We changed our minds more than once!

Best On-the-Go

Wims Unflavored

Sometimes the best THC drink isn’t technically a drink… yet.

Wims takes a different approach to the category with a tiny, portable packet of CBD + THC that turns whatever you’re already drinking into a buzzy beverage. Just snap, squeeze, and stir to instantly infuse your beverage of choice.

The unflavored format is the real win with this one. Instead of committing to somebody else’s favorite flavor, Wims Unflavored lets you enjoy your favorite drink—just with a little something extra.

Best Extra-Strength

Cornbread Hemp 10mg Raspberry Limeade THC Seltzer

Photography courtesy of Cornbread Hemp

Raspberry and lime is a hard combination to mess up… but it can also be a hard one to make memorable. Cornbread Hemp pulls it off with a fresh, juicy raspberry flavor and a burst of lime on the finish to keep each sip bright.

This is also where Cornbread turns up the dial. Each can packs 10mg of pure hemp-derived THC—twice the strength of its classic 5mg seltzers—making it a better fit for experienced THC drinkers or moments when 5mg just isn’t quite enough.

The flavor earned Silver at the High Spirits Awards, but the balance is what won us over. Stronger doesn’t have to mean harder to drink.

Most Unique Flavor

Cornbread Hemp Salted Watermelon THC Seltzer

Photography courtesy of Cornbread Hemp

Salt on watermelon is one of those combinations that sounds a little strange… until you try it.

Cornbread Hemp captures this sweet-and-salty summer classic surprisingly well in Salted Watermelon. Juicy watermelon is front and center, while a subtle salty finish keeps it bright and refreshing rather than sugary. It’s a smart combination for a sparkling drink, and one you won’t find anywhere else!

Another Gold medal winner at the High Spirits Awards, Salted Watermelon proves THC seltzer flavors can get interesting without getting weird.

Buy Here

Best Iced Tea

Cornbread Hemp Peach Iced Tea THC Seltzer

Photography courtesy of Cornbread Hemp

There’s something instantly familiar about Cornbread Hemp’s Peach Iced Tea. Ripe peach and fresh-brewed tea come together in a flavor that feels like a refreshing porch-sipper served over ice rather than a fruity seltzer.

With 5mg THC per can, it lands comfortably in everyday-sipping territory, with just enough sweetness to round out the tea without burying it. The flavor earned a Bronze medal at the High Spirits Awards, and we can see why! For anyone who likes their THC drinks refreshing, unfussy, and not too sweet, this one goes down easy.

Best for Mixing

Ellora THC Spirit Alternative

Not every THC drink needs to come in a can.

Ellora is built for people who love the ritual of making a cocktail as much as drinking one. Complex and botanical, this THC-infused spirit alternative gives you something to measure, pour, shake, and garnish… and it’s completely alcohol-free.

We especially like it in citrus-forward drinks, where its botanical character can stand up to fresh lemon or lime, herbs, and sparkling water instead of disappearing underneath them. If your bar cart is feeling neglected these days, Ellora gives the cocktail shaker a reason to come back out.

The Bottom Line

The best thing about THC drinks in 2026? It might be that there isn’t really one “best” way to drink them anymore!

Want something light and familiar? Crack open a 5mg seltzer. Prefer tea to fruity? There’s a can for that. Miss making cocktails? Grab a spirit alternative. Already know your THC tolerance and want something a little stronger? You’ve got options there, too.

That’s a good sign for the category. THC drinks aren’t just alternatives anymore… they’re becoming a beverage aisle all their own.

We’ll drink to that!
