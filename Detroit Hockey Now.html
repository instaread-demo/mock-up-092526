Should Red Wings Keep Both Johansson and ASP?

Young Swedish defenseman Anton Johansson has to be wondering whether his four-assist game Tuesday against Pittsburgh earned him a place on the Detroit Red Wings roster.

The answer to that question: Certainly. Likely. Probably. Maybe.

Unlike up front where Detroit doesn’t have enough scoring, the Red Wings are pleased with the options on the blue line. Once Simon Edvinsson is signed, the Red Wings have five desirable defensemen locked into spots. That group also includes Moritz Seider, Justin Faulk, Albert Johansson and Ben Chiarot.

The issue is the ranking of the defensemen who come next.

“We feel real good about our depth,” Detroit coach Todd McLellan said. “. . .there are players who have elevated their stock, for the lack of a better term, and Anton is one of those.”

The second issue is the Red Wings haven’t yet settled on how many defensemen to keep.

“Will we carry seven or eight?, ” McLellan said. “That’s a question that we internally have to continue to discuss.”

After sending veteran defenseman Jacob Bryson to Grand Rapids, the Red Wings now have Jacob-Bernard Docker, Axel Sandin-Pellikka, William Wallinder and Johansson now competing for two or three positions.

The Red Wings’ televised game tonight (7 p.m.) and Saturday’s home game are important for this group.

McLellan didn’t know Johansson well coming into training camp, because Johansson has had limited time with the Griffins.

Johansson Winning Over McLellan

“He has joy for the game,” McLellan said. “(Anton Johansson) loves being out there smiling. He brings an energy that kind of rubs off on other people.”

The Red Wings appreciate his two-way mindset and his physical inclinations.

“He’s aggressive killing plays, but he’s also aggressive with the puck in the offensive zone.”

The third factor that has to be considered is Ho don’t want a young player on the roster unless he is going to receive substantial playing. If these players would be in and out of the Detroit lineup, it would be better for their development to play 20-plus minutes for Grand Rapids.

Bernard-Docker has proven to be a useful veteran. He’s a competitive battler and McLellan seems to appreciate what he offers. Would interim GM Shawn Horcoff consider moving him to open up roster spots and playing time for youngsters?

Both Sandin-Pellikka and Johansson can be sent to Grand Rapids without going through waivers. Wallinder would have to go through waivers to be sent down.
