Best Novig Markets for Week 3 of NFL Sunday

This Sunday features a full slate of 14 NFL games. While a lot of player prop markets aren’t available yet, there’s some point spread markets I think are worth considering.

Las Vegas Raiders at New Orleans Saints

The Saints started with two tough road opponents in Detroit and Baltimore and almost came out 2-0. There’s a lot of positives going for New Orleans right now, so I expect a loud Superdome when they have their home opener against the Las Vegas Raiders. Here’s where the market stands today.

The Raiders are 2-0, which is a big deal for that franchise, but now it plays its first real road game in New Orleans and I think that environment will be tough for these Raiders to handle. The Saints have covered the spread in eight straight games, and I like them to do so on Sunday. With the current New Orleans Saints -3.5 Yes Contract below $0.50, it’s a great value play.

Los Angeles Chargers at Buffalo Bills

This game features two teams at completely opposite ends of the momentum spectrum. Buffalo is 2-0 and the top-rated team in the NFL by many pundits. Los Angeles is 0-2 and arguably the most disappointing team to start the 2026-27 season. Now they meet in Buffalo and the spread is heavily in favor of the Bills.

On paper, everything might be telling you that the Bills are going to roll, but I think the law of averages in the NFL comes into play here. The Bills have been getting hyped up by media and fans as being the Super Bowl favorite for two weeks, but they are still giving up 31 points per game. The Chargers have covered in four of their last five games as an underdog.

Los Angeles has been hearing about how awful it is for two weeks and many are questioning Justin Herbert and coach Jim Harbaugh. I think this is a good bounce-back spot for the Chargers offense. I like Herbert to have a strong game and keep his team in it. While Buffalo has looked unstoppable on offense, there really is nowhere for the offense to go but down and I think we see the Bills look a little more human in Week 3. I’ll take the Los Angeles Chargers +7.5 Yes Contract.
