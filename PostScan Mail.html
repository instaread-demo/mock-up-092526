World Cup 2026 may be over, but the U.S. stores, brands, and products you discovered during your trip are still only a few clicks away.

The harder part begins at checkout: many American retailers do not deliver to every country, and direct international shipping can be limited, expensive, or entirely unavailable for the specific product you want.

Fortunately, you can easily continue to shop from U.S. stores after World Cup 2026 by using a U.S. shopping address for international customers. Your purchases are delivered locally within the United States, added to your secure online mailbox, and can then be stored, consolidated, or forwarded to your home country based on your needs.

This guide walks you through exactly how to shop from U.S. stores from abroad, estimate the total landed cost of your items before buying, navigate common international shipping limitations and retailer restrictions. And leverage international package forwarding from the USA to manage your retail orders remotely without relying on hotels, short-term rentals, or family favors.

 

Can You Keep Shopping From U.S. Stores After Leaving the United States?

Yes. International customers can buy from eligible U.S. stores while living abroad. Provided the retailer accepts their localized payment method and has a valid domestic shipping destination to send the package to. A virtual mailbox provides a reliable U.S. delivery address for eligible purchases.

However, a U.S. shipping address does not mean every retail transaction goes through automatically. Payment gateways, online marketplaces, and customs authorities enforce strict rules.

Before placing your order, make sure you confirm these five checkpoints:

  1. Retailer Address Compatibility:
    The merchant accepts shipping to a Commercial Mail Receiving Agency (CMRA) without flagging it.
  2. Billing Verification:
    The merchant’s payment processor accepts your credit card, PayPal, or local payment method.
  3. Location Policies:
    The specific PostScan Mail location you selected is equipped to receive, handle, and store packages of that size or weight.
  4. Export Eligibility:
    The carrier you choose can legally transport the items out of the United States.
  5. Import Regulations:
    Your home country’s customs authority allows the physical contents to cross your local border.

 

Why Some U.S. Stores Do Not Ship Internationally

It can be frustrating when a major brand sells to customers nationwide but blocks international delivery.

This restriction is rarely due to a lack of interest in serving overseas fans; rather, Cross-border fulfillment involves additional shipping costs, customs requirements, taxes, and return-management challenges.

That many retailers choose to avoid:

  • High Carriage Rates:
    Managing international cargo lanes and small-parcel shipping across borders is costly without enterprise-level shipping volume.
  • Complex Duties & Classification:
    Calculating harmonized tariff codes, import duties, and country-specific taxes at checkout requires complex software integrations.
  • Geographic Distribution Deals:
    Exclusive brand licensing and regional distribution agreements often prohibit U.S. businesses from selling specific inventory outside their home territory.
  • Fraud Prevention Sweeps:
    Banks enforce strict anti-fraud rules that automatically flag transactions when the credit card’s billing country does not match the delivery address.
  • Regulatory Exclusions:
    Direct air shipping has incredibly strict rules regarding hazardous materials, lithium-ion batteries, liquid cosmetic formulas, and high-value precious metals.

A virtual mailbox provides a U.S. delivery option when the retailer accepts the address and the product is eligible for forwarding.

 

What Is a U.S. Virtual Shopping Address?

A U.S. virtual shopping address is a mailing address managed through an online mail and package-management service. Eligible purchases are delivered to the selected PostScan Mail location and added to your online account after processing.

When your online orders arrive at our Selected PostScan Mail location, our team digitizes the package’s shipping label and uploads a high-resolution image to your digital account.

From your smartphone, tablet, or laptop anywhere in the world, you can log into your dashboard and execute immediate, remote commands:

This workflow turns physical shipping logistics into a simple, software-driven experience, giving you remote access to available package-management actions.

Select a PostScan Mail address that supports your expected package volume and forwarding needs. If you are focused on e-commerce, select a location and plan based on package limits, storage periods, forwarding options, fees, and the services you expect to use.
Do not choose an address based only on the city or monthly subscription price.
Review your mailbox’s specific limits on incoming items, package storage, and available carriers before finalizing your choice.

Step 2: Complete USPS Form 1583

To legally authorize a mail receiving agency to handle your mail and packages, USPS requires customers receiving mail through a Commercial Mail Receiving Agency to complete Form 1583.
PostScan Mail also requires identity verification and notarization before it can handle eligible mail and packages on the customer’s behalf.

Step 3: Enter the Address Correctly at Checkout

Ensure you input your address exactly as it is formatted in your account.
Always include your unique mailbox or PMB (Private Mailbox) number. Omitting this identifier can delay package check-in at our facility.

Step 4: Confirm the Retailer Accepts the Order

Before making a high-value purchase, verify the store’s shipping policy regarding package forwarders.
Some restricted drops or highly regulated brands may have custom checkout screens to monitor and block commercial mail drops.

Step 5: Monitor the Package Online

Once the package has been processed, review its exterior image and any available item details in your online mailbox.
You can then choose from the actions available for that package and location.

Step 6: Store or Consolidate Your Purchases

If you are buying from different U.S. websites, let the packages accumulate in your mailbox. Once all items are checked in, request package consolidation to combine your items into a single, compact outbound box.
Consolidation may reduce packaging volume or the number of separate shipments.
Eligible orders may be consolidated when the service is available. Consolidation may reduce packaging volume or the number of shipments, but savings are not guaranteed.

Step 7: Select an International Shipping Option

Select your preferred shipping carrier (Review the carriers and service levels available for your package and destination, complete the required customs information, and submit your forwarding request), review the estimated delivery times, complete the digital customs declaration form, and forward your World Cup purchases straight to your doorstep.

 

Virtual Shopping Address vs. Direct International Shipping

Direct shipping is simple when a retailer handles all duties and offers flat-rate international delivery.

However, when those services are unavailable, or pricing is inflated, a virtual shopping address may be more practical if the retailer does not deliver internationally or if you want to manage purchases from several stores together.

Can You Combine Purchases From Different Stores?

Yes! If you purchased a jersey from one boutique, commemorative soccer balls from another, and limited-edition sneakers from an online marketplace, shipping them all separately is highly inefficient.

Carriers may calculate charges using actual weight or dimensional weight. The billable weight and applicable formula depend on the carrier, package, service, and destination.

By requesting package consolidation, our warehouse team will carefully unpack your multiple retail boxes, discard excess shipping materials, and bundle your items securely into a single optimized box.

 

Estimate International Shipping Costs Before You Buy

The product price is only one part of the purchase. Before ordering a large, heavy, fragile, or low-value item, estimate the cost of forwarding it from the selected PostScan Mail location to your destination.

The PostScan Mail Shipping Calculator allows shoppers to choose an origin location, enter a destination city or postal code, provide a package weight, and review estimated rates from available partner carriers.

Estimates can change after the final package dimensions, weight, packaging, destination details, or carrier surcharges are confirmed.

 

What Costs Should International Shoppers Consider?

Calculate the total landed cost rather than comparing only the retailer’s product price:

  • Product price
  • U.S. domestic delivery fee
  • Applicable sales tax
  • Mailbox subscription or processing fees
  • Storage fees (when the free period is exceeded)
  • Consolidation, repacking, or special-handling fees
  • International shipping charge
  • Insurance
  • Customs duties, import taxes, and clearance fees
  • Potential return or replacement shipping

Total landed cost = product price + U.S. delivery + service fees + international shipping + duties and taxes

A shipping-rate estimate does not calculate every customs charge. Duties and taxes are determined by the destination country and may depend on the product category, value, origin, and local import rules.

 

What Products May Be Restricted?

Certain products may be restricted, require special documentation, or be ineligible for forwarding. Eligibility depends on the specific product, carrier, route, and destination-country rules.

  • Products containing lithium batteries
  • Liquids, perfume, and aerosols
  • Food, supplements, and medicine
  • Alcohol and age-restricted products
  • Hazardous materials
  • Oversized packages
  • High-value merchandise
  • Products restricted by the destination country

 

What If a Store Rejects Your Virtual Address?

If your order is declined at checkout, do not panic or attempt to re-submit the transaction repeatedly,

which can flag your card for fraud. Instead:

  1. Verify Your Formatting:
    Ensure your name, street address, and PMB/Suite number are entered in the correct fields.
  2. Align Your Billing Address:
    Confirm that the shipping address and PMB number are correct. Verify that the billing information matches the records held by your card issuer. Contact the retailer to ask whether it accepts commercial receiving or forwarding addresses. Choose another authorized retailer when the store does not permit the transaction.
  3. Contact Support:
    Reach out to the store’s customer service. Often, a quick manual review by their team can white-list your virtual address once they verify you are an international customer.

 

How to Reduce Problems With Returns

International returns can cost more than the product. Reduce the risk before buying:

  • Read size charts and product measurements carefully.
  • Review the return deadline and final-sale exclusions.
  • Confirm whether personalized products can be returned.
  • Check warranty coverage outside the United States.
  • Keep invoices, receipts, and retailer messages.
  • Avoid forwarding an item you already know must be returned.
  • Compare the likely return cost with the product’s value.

For a full return workflow, read How to Manage World Cup 2026 Returns After Going Home.

Depending on the selected location and plan, PostScan Mail can provide:

  • Online notifications and package visibility after receipt
  • Temporary package storage

What a Virtual Shopping Address Cannot Guarantee

A virtual shopping address is a package-management tool, not a way to bypass retailer, payment, carrier, or customs rules.

It cannot guarantee:

  • Acceptance by every U.S. retailer or marketplace
  • Payment authorization or use as a billing address
  • Free U.S. domestic delivery
  • Lower total shipping costs for every order

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy from U.S. stores while living abroad?

Yes. By using a virtual mailbox, you get a real U.S. street address that you can enter as your shipping destination at checkout. When the retailer accepts the order, an eligible package can be delivered to your selected address and forwarded to a supported destination.

Can I use a virtual address as my billing address?

Not always. A virtual address is designed as a shipping destination. While A virtual shopping address is primarily a shipping destination. Billing-address approval is determined by the merchant, card issuer, and payment processor. Acceptance is determined solely by the merchant’s payment processor.

Can I shop from any U.S. store?

No. Retailer acceptance, payment requirements, product availability, geographic restrictions, and forwarding eligibility vary.

Can I combine orders from different stores?

Yes, our package consolidation service allows you to combine multiple incoming retail orders into a single outbound box, significantly reducing dimensional weight shipping fees.

How much does international package forwarding cost?

The price depends on actual or dimensional weight, package size, destination, carrier, delivery speed, packaging, insurance, and service fees. Use the shipping calculator for an estimate before placing a large order.

Will I pay customs duties or import taxes?

Possibly. Duties, taxes, and clearance fees depend on the destination country, declared value, product classification, origin, and local customs rules.

Can a replacement order be sent to my virtual address?

Yes, when the retailer accepts the address and the replacement package meets the receiving and forwarding requirements of the selected location and destination.

Do I need USPS Form 1583 for package receiving?

Yes. To legally receive and manage any mail or packages at a U.S. commercial address, the U.S. Postal Service requires a completed and notarized Form 1583. PostScan Mail provides an online process and an online-notary option. Processing and approval times may vary.
