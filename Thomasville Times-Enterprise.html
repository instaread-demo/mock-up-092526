Central flag football blanks Lowndes, Turner County

Published 11:45 am Thursday, September 24, 2026

By Clint Thompson

WILSON RACES DOWNFIELD: Sophomore Millie Wilson takes the ball down the field in Tuesday’s game against Lowndes County, where Central won 13-0. (Submitted Photo)

THOMASVILLE – Thomas County Central flag football is 4-0 on the season following another pair of shutout wins on Tuesday.

The Lady Yellow Jackets stuffed Lowndes 13-0 and throttled Turner County 48-0.

“I’m really happy with the girls and our coaches. We’ve still got some things to clean up, and honestly, will always have things to clean up,” Central coach Chad Parkerson said. “That’s the goal, to be better today than yesterday, every day. 

“Defensively, we are deep at rusher, and they are doing a great job in getting pressure, which is resulting in opportunities for the back end to get interceptions. Our linebackers are communicating, setting edges and pulling flags. We are playing a lot of people, which speaks to our depth. That will help us this year and in the future. I just want us to continue to improve.”

Combined with Central’s two shutout wins to open the season last week, the Lady Yellow Jackets have yet to allow a point this year.

Quarterback Kate Thedford sparked Central’s offense against Lowndes. She completed 8-of-22 passes and rushed for two touchdowns. Lexi Frysz led Central’s defense with six flags pulled. Kaaliyah Brooks added four flags and an interception.

Thedford shined again against Turner County, completing 9-of-17 passes for 76 yards and four touchdowns. Frysz and Ava Bishop each recorded a touchdown. Central converted 4-of-5 drives into scores and did not commit a turnover.

Qua’Mya Yearby led the Lady Yellow Jackets’ defense with three flags pulled and one interception. E’Zhara Cromartie pulled two flags and recorded one interception, while Wynter Sessions pulled two flags and registered one sack.
